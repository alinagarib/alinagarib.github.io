     INTRO
    </legend>
    <p>Hello Friends. Thank you for subscribing to my newsletter and I hope this email finds you well. I am looking forward to keeping in touch, and I encourage you to write to me back as well :P. I hope you all are well. It is weird but also very beautiful and exciting how all my friends are now spread apart and on their own journeys. I am so excited to see where life goes for everyone, and how the future unfolds.  
    </p>
    <p>
I often think about how the internet is this resource that is open for anyone to create on, but most do not. When social media first came about, it was such a novel place for self expression, individuality, and monoculture across internet users. The internet used to be utilized with intention, such as picking who you subscribe to on YouTube and browsing your subscriptions for something to watch. The explore page was whatever was going viral. This fostered an actual sense of community within the online world, as there was a distinct monoculture which people could consume and adhere to, and references which were made and understood across everyone on the platform. There was also this sense of agency, and the distinct purpose of keeping up with certain content creators and your friends. Modern algorithms have removed this sense of agency and going on social media with the specific purpose of keeping up with someone, or something. The advancement of algorithms have removed all decision making and intentionality of what we consume. When these platforms gained significant user bases, corporations in charge realized that they have control. They can feed information to their users in a way that makes them stay on the app, in a way that helps them sell products. I feel like we are in an era of dopamine wars, and I am certainly a victim as I am sure most of us are. But I want to focus on reclaiming what makes the internet such a unique thing, and how behind all the algorithms and corporate hypnotization, it was originally a place for people to create. To be themselves, to access information and share it like never before. The internet still exists as a relatively unregulated entity that is available to the people, and we should take advantage of that. It is not that we don’t have the ability to, but we have neglected it as an option in favor of convenience.  
</p>

<p>
That is my long prelude as to why I am excited to get this started, and create and share intentionally. I hope to be inconvenienced more frequently, as to be inconvenienced is to master process over craft. And I hope to be inconvenienced by others, as inconvenience is connection and  meaningful sacrifice for others. And although these letters may be long, I hope they find you, inconvenienced. </p>

<p>
The biggest life change I have gone through recently is accepting my return offer at Bloomberg. I am due to start June 21st and will be moving to the city in June! It was a little strange not participating in any recruitment or career fair events this year and I almost felt FOMO. I was about to go to a Google networking event, which would have been useful regardless, but I didn’t because I felt like I was in a corporate psychosis for even considering it. Like I should be happy that I already have a job and actually relax. So I didn’t go, but I do slightly regret it, idk. I guess it is hard to reprogram. But I am so thrilled that I got the return offer, and I am very much looking forward to starting the rest of my life and being in the city, which I've wanted for years, and at a company which treats me well, has interesting people, and offers the flexibility for me to have a life. It is honestly everything I could have ever asked for and I live in fear that I am going to die of a freak accident because stuff is going too well in my favor. I really hope to keep making it another day without getting impaled in the chest or getting run over by a car or something.  
</p>

<p>
Another job related update is that my TA job for Intro to Software Engineering rehired me. They fired all the undergraduate TA’s in the CS department right after the semester started, realized it caused major issues, and hired us back in like two weeks. I am super glad to be working this job again because I genuinely really enjoy teaching the students and it keeps me thinking about software design and the engineering process. I love to see what my students come up with and the type of insight I can provide for them. It is also really nice to have some spending money, since I had no intention of returning to my part-time internship this semester because it is pretty demanding time wise. 
</P>

<p>
My increased amount of free time is a good segue into my next life updates, which is what I have been doing with my newfound free time lately. My last 2-3 years of college and I guess just my life, I have had a pretty busy schedule which left me with little time to actually have any hobbies or do stuff that I enjoy, especially because in moments of free time there was always something I could be doing. Now I care a little less about performing in my classes since I am employed, and my classes are kind of bullshit. So as a newfound person who must not think all the time, I have continued with my knitting project which I hope to finish by the next newsletter. I started this one over summer and it is definitely taking me longer than it should. I keep trying to take it with me places and end up tangling the yarn which discourages me from continuing since it takes so long to untangle. <i>[ Sidebar I think about how much of my life I spend untangling things and it is actually quite high (wired earbuds, yarn, hair, I definitely spend at least 1 hr a week untangling, which isn't much but feels like a lot for untangling.) ]</i>
</p>

<p>
 Ava and I have started a cat puzzle. We are about 1/3rd of the way there, which I also hope to be done with by the next newsletter. I have been playing the piano a lot more, and it is cool how your brain just remembers things that were reinforced at a young age. Like I don’t feel intimidated by the difficulty of a piece anymore, just aware that it will take more time. It has been really nice to reconnect with this. My focus now is to continue learning more pieces which I enjoy, and start to get a better understanding of chords, keys, and the theory behind piano which never stuck for me. Ava also plays the drums, so we have been playing to back Irie, a really great singer and guitarist. This is my first experience playing with other instruments, and it is really fun. We have been playing at open mics and it is helping me get over my stage fright, which has been one of my goals for a while. 
 </p>
